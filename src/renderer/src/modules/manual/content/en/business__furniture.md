# Furniture

## What's different about this business type

Furniture is rarely a walk-out sale — most orders are booked with a deposit, made to order or customized (fabric, colour, dimensions, finish), and delivered and installed later once ready, with the balance collected on delivery. Furniture also turns on old-item trade-in, the same discount-folding mechanism Jewellery uses for old-metal exchange.

## Furniture Bookings

Open **Furniture Bookings** in the sidebar to start a booking: pick the customer, add each item with its quantity and price, and optionally capture the made-to-order customization for a line — fabric, colour, dimensions, or finish. Set an advance amount and how it was collected; the advance can't exceed the booking's total.

When the order is ready, **Generate Invoice** on the booking — this creates the real invoice from the booking's own items (not a placeholder line) and automatically records the already-collected advance as a real payment against it, capped at the invoice total. The booking is marked Delivered.

**Cash Flow Forecast** appears automatically at the top of the Furniture Bookings screen: every still-booked, not-yet-invoiced order's balance due (total minus advance already collected) is projected by expected delivery month, so you can see how much cash is coming in and roughly when — orders with no delivery date yet are grouped separately as unscheduled.

## Old-Item Trade-In

Open **Furniture Trade-Ins** to record a customer trading in an old piece against a new purchase. Enter an item description, condition, and a shop-assessed trade-in value (there's no market-rate lookup the way Jewellery's metal exchange has — furniture value is a judgment call, not a formula). While billing that customer, apply the trade-in the same way an old-metal exchange or gift-card credit is applied: it folds straight into the invoice's discount and is marked used so it can't accidentally be applied twice.

## Reports

Alongside the standard Sales, Inventory, and Financial reports, Furniture gets:

- **Showroom-vs-Warehouse Stock** — a per-product breakdown of stock by Location (see the Inventory Valuation & Multi-Location Stock chapter for how Locations work) — set up a "Showroom" and a "Warehouse" Location and this report shows exactly how much of each item sits in each, rather than only one combined total.
- **Delivery & Installation Schedule** — every furniture booking with a delivery date in the selected range, its address, status, and value, as a real schedule you can work off rather than hunting through the bookings list.

## Language

Furniture is not one of Sarang's service-business templates — it's a product-category business type, so it is **not** language-locked. The core interface is available in all 13 supported languages.
