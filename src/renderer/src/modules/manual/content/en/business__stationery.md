# Stationery

## What's different about this business type

A stationery shop sells single retail items over the counter, but a large share of real revenue comes from bulk institutional orders — a school's booklist, an office's annual supply order — matched against a named list and billed in one shot, plus in-shop print/photocopy/binding services billed alongside physical products.

## Bulk-List Orders

Open **Bulk-List Orders** in the sidebar to start one: give it a list name (e.g. "Grade 5 Booklist 2026-27") and either a customer record or a free-text institution name, then enter each line from the supply list as a raw label and requested quantity — you don't need to know which catalog product it maps to yet.

Once entered, match each line to a real catalog product and a price. An order can only be billed once **every** line is matched — the order card shows which lines still need matching. Billing generates one real invoice covering the whole order in a single action, against whichever payment method you choose (including Credit for an institutional account).

**Annual Reorder Reminders** appear automatically at the top of the Bulk-List Orders screen: institutions tend to reorder on a roughly annual cycle, so once an institution's last billed order passes 10 months old it's flagged **Due Soon**, and past 14 months, **Overdue** — a nudge to reach out before they go looking elsewhere.

## Print / Copy Services

While billing, use **Add Print / Copy Service** to add a line for B&W Print, Color Print, Photocopy, Binding, or Lamination alongside whatever physical products are in the cart — pick the service, set a quantity and price per unit, and it's added as its own cart line, billed and printed on the invoice exactly like a product.

## Kit Bundling

Stationery products can use Sarang's general kit-bundling feature (see the Inventory chapter) to sell a pre-built combo — a "Back to School Kit" made of several individual items — as one priced product that still deducts real stock from each component when sold.

## Reports

Alongside the standard Sales, Inventory, and Financial reports, Stationery gets:

- **Seasonal Demand Forecast** — every sale ever recorded, aggregated by calendar month across all years, so you can see which months are genuinely your busiest (school reopening, exam season) and plan stock-up ahead of the next occurrence rather than reacting to it.
- **Institutional Order History** — every past bulk-list order with its status, item count, and total value, for renewal planning and to see an institution's ordering pattern over time.

## Language

Stationery is not one of Sarang's service-business templates — it's a product-category business type, so it is **not** language-locked. The core interface is available in all 13 supported languages.
