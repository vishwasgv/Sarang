# Start Here: Where Everything Is

This page is the map. Read it once and you will always know where to go for a job, and what each word in Sarang means.

## The screen at a glance

- **Left sidebar** — every screen, grouped into modules (below). Click a group's title to fold it away or open it; Sarang remembers your choice. The group that holds the screen you are on always opens by itself, and only one item is highlighted at a time.
- **Top bar** — **Search** (press **Ctrl + K** from anywhere to jump to a customer, product, invoice or screen), the **bell** (app alerts), and your name (language, theme, sign out).
- **Bell alerts** — low stock, backup done, reminders due, and similar. Click an alert and Sarang opens the screen it is about (for example, a low-stock alert opens Inventory). An alert with a small "Open →" line is clickable.
- **Ask Sarang** — the sparkle item at the top of the sidebar, and the box at the top of the Dashboard. Type a question in plain English. The first time, it asks you to press **Turn on Ask Sarang**; after that it answers from your own data, on this computer.

## The sidebar modules

What you see depends on your business type and on what your login is allowed to do. The groups are always in this order.

| Group | What lives here |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Dashboard, Ask Sarang |
| **Sales** | Billing (invoices and the sale screen), Quotations, Sales Orders, Sales Returns (if turned on), Credit Notes, Customers, Price Lists, Pricing Schemes, Price Markdowns, Loyalty Program |
| **Purchases** | Suppliers, Purchase Orders, GRN (goods received note, if Logistics is on), Supplier Bills, Supplier Payments, Debit Notes |
| **Inventory** | Products, Inventory (stock levels and movements), Stock Locations, Print Labels, Batch Tracking, Serial Tracking |
| **Business Tools** | Everything specific to your kind of business: tables and kitchen for a restaurant, appointments for a clinic, metal rates for a jeweller, job cards for a repair shop, and so on. Your business-type chapter in this Manual describes each one. |
| **Accounting** | Expenses, Cash Close, Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries, Bank Accounts, Post-Dated Cheques, Bank Deposits, Fixed Assets, Ledger Settings, Cost Centres, Budgets, Recurring Profiles, Approval Workflows |
| **People & Payroll** | Employees, Staff Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Staff Commission |
| **Reminders & Messages** | WhatsApp Reminders, Message Templates |
| **Logistics** | Fleet, Carriers, Shipments, Delivery Challan, Freight Ledger (only if Logistics is on) |
| **Manufacturing** | Raw Materials, Bill of Materials, Production and related (only for manufacturers) |
| **Reports & Data** | Reports, Documents, Import Data, Backup, Audit Log |
| **Settings & Help** | Settings, Manual, About |

If you cannot find a screen, it is one of three things: your business type does not use it, your login does not have permission (ask the owner — see *Users & Permissions*), or the feature is switched off (**Settings → Additional Business Features**).

## Words that mean one thing each

Sarang keeps the sales side and the buying side clearly separate. These are the terms, in pairs.

| You are... | Selling to a customer | Buying from a supplier |
|---|---|---|
| Asking for a price | **Quotation** (you quote them) | (a supplier's quote is not recorded; go straight to a Purchase Order) |
| Confirming an order | **Sales Order** | **Purchase Order** |
| Goods moving | **Delivery Note / Delivery Challan** (you send goods out) | **Receive Stock** on the Purchase Order, or a **GRN** (goods come in) |
| The money document | **Invoice** (made in **Billing**) | **Supplier Bill** (the supplier's invoice to you) |
| Money moving | **Payment received** (recorded on the invoice; see Payment History) | **Supplier Payment** (recorded on the bill) |
| Returning goods or correcting an amount | **Sales Return** and **Credit Note** (you owe the customer) | **Debit Note** (the supplier owes you; this is your purchase return) |
| The person or business | **Customer** (shown as *Patient* or *Owner* in clinics) | **Supplier** |
| Who owes whom | **Outstanding** = what customers owe you | **Balance payable** = what you owe suppliers |

Other terms you will meet:

- **Stock Location** — a place where stock is kept (shop, godown, van). It is not an address.
- **Staff Attendance** — your employees' attendance. **Student Attendance** exists only for coaching institutes; **Customer Check-In** only where walk-in check-in is turned on.
- **Raw Material Suppliers** (manufacturers) — the suppliers you buy raw materials from; they are also in **Suppliers**.
- **Document Builder** — design your own printed forms. **Documents** — file storage for scans and attachments.
- **Reminder** — a message Sarang prepares for you to send on WhatsApp. **Alert** (bell) — a note to you inside the app. They are different: alerts are for you, reminders are for your customers.

## Where do I start? (a 30-minute first day)

1. **Settings → Business Profile**: business name, address, tax number, logo.
2. **Products**: add what you sell (or import a list — **Import Data**). Set the tax rate on each.
3. **Customers** and **Suppliers**: add your regular ones, or import them.
4. **Billing**: make your first sale. Print it or send it on WhatsApp.
5. **Purchase Orders → Receive Stock** (or **Supplier Bills**): record your first purchase.
6. **Reports → Sales** and the **Dashboard**: see your first numbers.

Then read the guides that match your day: *Guide: Selling*, *Guide: Buying*, *Guide: Products and Stock*, *Guide: Tax and GST*, *Guide: Money and Your Books*, and *Guide: Daily, Weekly and Monthly Routine*.
