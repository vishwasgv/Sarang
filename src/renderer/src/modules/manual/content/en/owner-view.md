# Owner View (Phone Dashboard)

## What it is

Owner View lets you check your business's numbers from **your own phone or tablet**, without touching the desktop computer — useful when your accountant or cashier is busy billing and you just want a quick look at today's sales, outstanding, or inventory. It's **read-only**: nothing can be created, edited, billed, or deleted from a phone. You're looking, not touching.

Owner View runs entirely over your **own shop Wi-Fi** — it works the same way Sarang's Doctor Pad, Kitchen Display, and Field Order Capture features do. Nothing ever goes over the internet and nothing is sent to Aszurex or anyone else; your phone simply talks directly to the same computer Sarang is already running on.

## How to turn it on

Owner View is **off by default**. Turn it on from **Settings → Appearance → Owner View — phone / tablet (read-only)**.

Once enabled, you'll see one or more addresses to open in your phone's browser, plus a **QR code** you can scan and a **6-digit access code** you can type instead if scanning isn't convenient (say, your camera is dirty or the light is bad). Either way, you only need to connect once — after that, your phone remembers the connection and there's no need to scan or type anything again.

- **Show QR code** — displays (and lets you print) a QR code your phone's camera can scan.
- **Or type this code once** — a plain 6-digit number you can type into your phone's browser at the address shown, instead of scanning.
- **Regenerate link** / **Regenerate code** — if a phone that should no longer have access does (for example, an old device you no longer use), regenerating either one cuts off any device that hasn't reconnected since — already-connected devices you still use keep working normally.

**Important:** your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer. Sarang runs fully offline, so Owner View cannot be reached from mobile data or a different Wi-Fi network.

## What you can see

Once connected, your phone shows:

- **Today at a glance** — the same key numbers as the desktop Dashboard: today's/this week's/this month's sales (with trend arrows), outstanding receivable, inventory value, estimated profit, low-stock count, and customer count.
- **Reports** — a real, live set of business reports you can open and read on your phone: Sales, Outstanding (Receivable & Payable), Inventory, Tax Summary, Expenses, Profit & Loss, Cash Book, Trial Balance, Accounts Payable Aging, and Purchase Register. Reports that cover a date range let you pick your own **From**/**To** dates right on your phone; the rest show your business's current position with no date needed.

This is a starting set of the most commonly checked reports — not yet every report the desktop Reports screen offers. If there's a specific report you'd like added to Owner View, this is a good thing to tell Aszurex about.

## What it won't do

Owner View never creates, changes, bills, or deletes anything — there is no "edit" or "save" anywhere in it, by design. You cannot create an invoice, record a payment, adjust stock, or change any setting from a phone through Owner View. If you need to make a change, do it on the desktop as usual.

## Language

Owner View's phone screen currently displays in **English only**, regardless of which language you've set for the rest of Sarang's interface.
