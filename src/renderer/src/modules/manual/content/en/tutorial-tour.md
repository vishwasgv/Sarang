# Guided Tutorial

Sarang includes a guided, step-by-step walkthrough of the app — a real, working copy of Sarang for a business type you pick, not a set of static screenshots. You click through actual screens and try actual actions (creating an invoice, booking an appointment, and so on), and every step tells you exactly what to do and try.

## Starting the tutorial for the first time

The first time you finish setting up Sarang and reach the Dashboard, you're asked: **"Want a quick guided tour of Sarang?"** Choosing **Yes, show me around** lets you pick between exploring your own business type or a different one, then starts the tour. Choosing **Maybe later** simply dismisses the prompt — it never appears again on its own, but the tutorial is always available later from the two places below.

## Coming back to it later

You can start (or restart) the tutorial at any time, from either of these:

- **The "?" icon in the top bar** — available on every screen, opens the same tutorial picker instantly.
- **Settings → Tutorial** — shows the same picker, plus a one-click **Restart Tutorial for [Your Business Type]** button.

Either way, you first choose whether to explore your own business type or a different one, so you can see what Sarang looks like for a Restaurant, a Clinic, a Jewellery store, and so on, even if that isn't your business.

## What the tour actually covers

Every screen your business type has gets a real, navigable step — nothing is skipped. The most distinctive screens for each business (KOT and Tables for a Restaurant, Metal Rates and Exchange for Jewellery, Blood Issue for a Blood Bank, learner sessions for a Driving School, and so on) get detailed, hand-written guidance walking you through exactly what to try. Screens that are common to every business type (Purchase Orders, HR, Cash Close, and similar) get a simpler "here's this screen, look around" step rather than a full scripted walkthrough of every button — the *Billing*, *Inventory*, *Reports*, and other chapters of this Manual cover those in full detail.

## Nothing here is ever saved

While the tutorial is running, a banner reading **"Tutorial Mode — nothing here is saved"** stays visible at all times, and **Exit Tutorial** is always one click away. Every invoice, booking, or record you create during the tour lives in a separate, throwaway copy of Sarang — it never touches your real business data, and it is discarded automatically the moment you exit or finish the tour. You can safely click, type, and submit anything the tour asks you to, exactly as if it were your real data, with zero risk.

## Skipping or leaving early

**Skip Tour** is available at every step if you'd rather explore on your own, and **Back**/**Next** let you move at your own pace. Leaving the tutorial early (or closing Sarang mid-tour) is completely safe — nothing is left half-saved, and you can always restart from the beginning later using either entry point above.
