# Cash Close

Open **Cash Close** from the sidebar at the end of a shift or day to reconcile the physical cash in your drawer against what Sarang expects based on the payments actually recorded that day.

## Reading the summary

Pick a date (defaults to today) to see every payment method's total collected — Cash, UPI, Card, Wallet, Credit — and the **Expected Cash** figure this produces: the cash portion of what was actually collected, calculated straight from your recorded payments.

## Recording the close

Count the physical cash in your drawer and enter it under **Cash in Drawer**. As soon as you type an amount, Sarang shows the **Variance** — the difference between what you counted and what was expected — labelled **Balanced** (within a rounding cent), a plain over/short if the gap is small, or **Significant** over/short if it's more than ₹100, so a real discrepancy stands out at a glance rather than blending in with normal rounding noise. Add an optional note (e.g. "Opened with ₹500 float, dropped ₹1000 in safe") for context, then tap **Record Close**.

## Re-closing a date

Sarang keeps exactly one cash-close record per date. Opening a date that's already been closed shows a banner with when it was recorded, and pre-fills the form with what you entered before so you can review or correct it. Tapping the button again (now labelled **Update Close**) asks you to confirm first, since it overwrites that date's previous actual cash and variance — a real financial record, not something to silently replace.

## Recent closes

The table at the bottom shows your last 10 cash closes — date, expected, actual, variance, and your note — as a quick reference without needing to open each date individually.
