# Plumbing

## What's different about this business type

A plumbing supplier sells pipe cut to length off a coil or length the same way Electrical sells wire, alongside piece-counted fittings and fragile sanitaryware that often needs a scheduled delivery rather than a walk-out counter sale. Plumbing also turns on job-site running accounts for contractors and warranty tracking on fixtures — with a way to re-attribute that warranty once a fixture is actually installed at a customer's site.

## Pipe-length billing

Works exactly like Electrical's meter-based wire billing — turn on **Sell by Length** on a pipe product, choose a unit and price per unit, and it bills by length at checkout rather than by piece, with a fine-grained quantity input for exact cut lengths.

## Job-Site Accounts

Identical to Electrical's Job-Site Accounts (see that chapter, or open **Job-Site Accounts** in the sidebar) — a running account for a contractor working one site, tagged onto CREDIT invoices at billing time, with a per-account view of every tagged invoice and the running outstanding balance.

## Scheduled Delivery

For fragile sanitaryware that shouldn't just be handed over the counter, billing supports an optional **Scheduled Delivery** — set a delivery date and address while creating the invoice, and it appears in **Scheduled Deliveries** in the sidebar. From there, move a delivery through **Scheduled → Out for Delivery → Delivered**, or cancel it, as it actually happens.

## Installation Warranty Transfer

A fixture's warranty is usually attributed to whoever's invoice it was sold on — but the buyer (often a contractor on a job-site account) and the person who'll actually live with the fixture long enough to ever need to claim that warranty (the homeowner) are frequently different people. Open **Serial Tracking**, use the **Installation Warranty Transfer** search to find a sold unit by its serial number, then transfer the warranty to the customer it was actually installed for, with an optional installation address. Only a unit that's already been sold can have its warranty transferred.

## Reports

Alongside the standard Sales, Inventory, and Financial reports, Plumbing gets:

- **Fitting Compatibility Cross-Sell** — the same basket-composition analysis Retail uses to find what's commonly bought together, read for plumbing fittings: which products actually get bought in the same basket, so you know what to bundle or suggest.
- **Material Sales Mix** — revenue and units broken down by product category (pipes, fittings, sanitaryware, or however you've categorized your catalog), so you can see at a glance where the money's coming from.

## Language

Plumbing is not one of Sarang's service-business templates — it's a product-category business type, so it is **not** language-locked. The core interface is available in all 13 supported languages.
