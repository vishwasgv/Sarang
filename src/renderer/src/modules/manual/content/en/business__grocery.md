# Grocery / Kirana Store

## What's different about this business type

A Grocery/Kirana store sells a high volume of short-shelf-life goods (batch/expiry tracking on by default), extends running "khata" credit to regular customers, and often sells staples like grains, pulses, and oil loose by weight rather than pre-packaged. Grocery combines Pharmacy's batch/expiry tracking with Distributor's credit-limit and outstanding-analytics modules — a proven combination, not a new one.

## Khata (Credit) Auto-Reminder

Open the **Outstanding** report — any customer carrying an overdue khata balance gets a **Khata Risk Tier** report of their own (see below) with a one-tap **Send Reminder** button next to their name. Pressing it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message stating their outstanding balance, and records when the reminder was sent so the same customer isn't reminded again for at least 7 days. As with every WhatsApp share in Sarang, the app hands off to WhatsApp and cannot confirm the message was actually sent — it's on you to press send.

## Loose (Weight-Based) Billing

Loose billing isn't Grocery-exclusive — it's a per-product toggle available to any business type (see **Product → Sell by Weight**). For a Kirana store it's how grains, pulses, and oil are typically priced: set a price per kilogram/litre on the product, and the billing screen bills by weight entered at the counter instead of a fixed per-unit price.

## Reports

Alongside the standard Sales, Inventory, and Financial reports, Grocery gets:

- **MRP Compliance** — every past sale line where the unit price exceeded the product's printed MRP, with the excess collected — a real compliance check, not just a reference number.
- **Perishable Wastage** — stock written off as expired (use the **Expiry** reason when adjusting stock for expired goods), by product and value.
- **Daily Restock Alert** — fast-selling products running low on stock, ranked by how many days of stock are left at the current sales pace.
- **Loose vs. Packaged Sales Mix** — how much of your revenue comes from loose (weight-billed) goods versus pre-packaged SKUs.
- **Khata Risk Tier** — every credit customer ranked by risk, combining how overdue their oldest debt is with whether their balance has been rising or falling over the last 30 days — flags a regular sliding toward bad debt before they actually default, not just a static balance list.

## Language

Grocery is not one of Sarang's service-business templates — it's a product-category business type, so it is **not** language-locked. The core interface is available in all 13 supported languages.
